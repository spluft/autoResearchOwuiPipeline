# Research Completion Criteria — gates A–F

Evaluate gates explicitly and report them. If a gate cannot be satisfied, explain
why — stating an honest "Gate C partial: primary spec paywalled" beats manufactured
green ticks.

## Gate A — Scope

- Original question restated; boundaries (time, version, domain, geography) set.
- Ambiguities resolved or flagged; forbidden assumptions listed.
- Fail-closed check: could you answer the WRONG question confidently? If that's
  possible, you're not through A.

## Gate B — Coverage

- Every research area from the plan is `evidence-OK` or carries a documented gap
  with impact assessment ("gap in area C could change conclusion? yes/no").
- No material sub-question was discovered late and silently dropped.
- Adaptive: coverage is judged against the plan, NOT against a source-count target.
  Six well-chosen sources closing all areas beat fifteen unsorted ones.

## Gate C — Evidence

- Every critical conclusion is supported by fetched primary sources or ≥2
  independent fetched secondary sources (see source-evaluation.md).
- Search snippets were not treated as evidence anywhere.
- Critical single-source claims are downgraded to medium and visibly marked.
- Deep mode hardening: at least one primary source inspected per critical claim, or
  an explicit, defended exception (domain where no primary exists).

## Gate D — Contradictions

- Contradiction register filled — or a recorded statement that reliable sources
  converge and no material dispute exists (don't fabricate controversy).
- Each real contradiction: positions with sources+dates, independence assessed,
  resolution stated or non-resolution declared.
- At least one falsification attempt was actually run in deep mode (name the query).

## Gate E — Verification

- Fact-check pass executed (checklist in fact-checking.md) and its outcome reported:
  items checked, failures, corrections/downgrades applied.
- Spot-verify: open two random report citations; each cited URL was really fetched
  this session and contains the support.

## Gate F — Completion

- Report structure per template; every conclusion traceable to a ledger row.
- Residual uncertainty transparent: limitations, inaccessible sources, what would
  change the answer, unresolved questions with next steps.
- Date-stamped: "research verified through <date>".
- Epistemic tags intact (no FACT that is really CLAIM(source)).

## Mode-specific expectations (adaptive thresholds, not source quotas)

- **Quick:** A, E (light: spot-check the single fact), F (concise) — answer + citation,
  primary preferred. B/C/D satisfied trivially by question shape; if not, upgrade.
- **Standard:** A–F; coverage across all areas; at least one independent
  corroboration of the main finding; contradictions where they exist.
- **Deep:** A–F with hardenings above (primary per critical claim, falsification
  round, explicit fact-check report, ledger shown or summarized).

## Budget exhaustion rule

When mode budget runs out: do not overrun silently, and do not stop cold. State the
budget hit, list open gaps with what each missing search would test, and either
(a) conclude with documented gaps, or (b) ask the user whether to extend. A
budget-limited honest report beats an unlimited unbounded one.
